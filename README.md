# Traveler-s-Marketplace
This project is a fictional, highly stylized front-end website designed to look and feel like a long-lost digital artifact. Think of it as a dusty, creaky website from the ancient lost history that was buried deep in a forgotten server, holding secrets, memories, and "dangerous" knowledge from the ancient past. <br>
I built this to experiment with rich, moody texturing, antique typography, and responsive layouts combined with some modern looking website feeling.
<br> <br>
# What’s Inside?
The website is split into three main pages, each serving a specific (and highly classified) purpose: <br> <br>
• The Market: A place to browse forbidden artifacts, cursed relics, and items that definitely shouldn't be for sale. (Don't worry, the checkout button doesn't actually summon anything... I think). <br>
• Memories: A digital archive of lost thoughts, forgotten history, and journal entries from people who vanished centuries ago. It's beautiful, a little haunting, and entirely made up. <br>
• Heirlooms: A showcase of powerful family treasures and ancient objects. Each item has its own backstory, details, and warnings about why you probably shouldn't touch it. ( its still under construction ) <br> <br>
